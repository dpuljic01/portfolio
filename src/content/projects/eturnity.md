Eturnity builds software for solar energy planning and simulation. I worked as a backend engineer building and maintaining Django services that powered energy calculation pipelines and internal tooling used by the operations and sales teams.

The core challenge was a set of tightly coupled services with poor visibility into failures — incidents were slow to diagnose and slower to resolve. I introduced structured logging, centralised error reporting, and clear service boundaries that made root-cause analysis measurably faster.

I also built internal tooling that automated several manual data-processing workflows, reducing the operational burden on non-technical staff. All services ran on AWS with Docker-based deployments managed through a CI/CD pipeline.
